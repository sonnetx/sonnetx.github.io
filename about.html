About

Welcome to my corner of the internet. I'm Sonnet Xu, and this is where I share my thoughts on various topics.

I believe in the power of clear thinking and clear writing. This blog is my attempt to practice both—to work through ideas in public and hopefully contribute something useful to the conversation.

My background is pretty computer science heavy. I'm particularly interested in LLMs, AI ethics, and you'll find me writing about these topics occasionally.

When I'm not writing, you can find me reading a memoir or eating brunch. I'm always interested in connecting with thoughtful people, so feel free to reach out.

This blog is intentionally minimal. I hope you find something here that resonates with you.
